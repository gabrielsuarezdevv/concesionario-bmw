# Leather detail maps

Leather White by Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven:
https://polyhaven.com/a/leather_white

License: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Unmodified 1024×1024 JPEG normal (OpenGL) and roughness maps downloaded from:
https://api.polyhaven.com/files/leather_white

The viewer uses the maps as surface detail with configurable upholstery colours.
No diffuse photograph, generated interior image or baked panoramic view replaces the 3D model.
